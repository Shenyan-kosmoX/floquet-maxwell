# Native dynamic spectral-response maps for Fig. 4d/g

Both maps are generated by live native Meep D/B stepping with time-varying sphere permittivity. The physical square lattice has R/a=1/3, a=631.8 nm, air outside, epsilon0=11.68, and modulation amplitude m=0.01 of chi0=10.68: epsilon(t)=11.68+0.1068 ramp(t) cos(2*pi*fm*t+pi/2). The drives are fixed across the entire M-Gamma-X-M path: 183 THz for the nonresonant map and 392 THz for the resonant map. The latter correspond exactly to the paper's stated pumps; no local frequency retuning is used in these global maps.

Each map contains 49 actual k points, 16 intervals per path segment, with vertices at indexes [0,16,32,48]. Geometry is native voxel r12, epsilon averaging disabled, Courant 0.5, padding 3a and PML thickness 1a, with no mirror symmetry. Displaced Hz and Ez sources and three probes recording all six E/H components expose both z parities subject to source/probe visibility. This is not a certification of complete TE+TM modes.

The broad pulse has center f*a/c=0.38 and width 0.60. After the source ends, the static system evolves for 20a/c. Modulation then turns on with a ten-pump-period sin-squared ramp. The subsequent 100 pump periods provide the spectral record. Materials are rebuilt through public set_materials every four Maxwell steps with midpoint sampling for the next four D evaluations. Fields are sampled every Maxwell step: dt=1/24 a/c, Nyquist=12 c/a. This exceeds the largest retained signed sideband range, +/-4fm; even the larger drive gives 4fm about 3.3045 c/a.

Raw files `regime/kXX/raw_dynamic_fields.npz` preserve complex E/H, times, material updates, coordinates, components, and the exact drive. `dynamic_spectrum.npz` preserves the **complete signed unfolded FFT**, in addition to folded powers for both Hann and end-only taper windows. Physical signed frequency equals minus numpy.fftfreq because fields follow exp(-i*2*pi*f*t). Neither the positive nor the negative frequency half is fabricated by symmetry.

Folding is an incoherent bin sum of the actually evolved signed spectral power for |f|<4fm into nu=(f/fm) modulo 1. FFT display samples are Riemann-weighted by their actual df, and the result is divided by the physical folded bin width. A saved integral check verifies that integrated folded power equals integrated retained unfolded power. The common folded grid contains 256 bins on [0,1). No static response is substituted or copied to make Floquet replicas.

This observable is a finite-time, source-visible dynamic probe response. Per-k normalization is only for display. It is **not** the paper's T-matrix Smin, a complete Floquet eigenfrequency spectrum, an energy density, a QNM/residue metric, or a proof of temporal growth. A 100-period record has independent resolution roughly 0.01 of the drive, far too coarse to resolve the narrow nonresonant gap. The existing long local dynamic trajectories provide separate gap and gain evidence. Growth, decay and window weighting affect FFT intensities; linewidths cannot be interpreted as Im omega or Q.

The r12 spatial grid, finite pad3/PML1, sample-and-hold material approximation, harmonic truncation |f|<4fm, finite record and source/probe visibility remain explicit limitations. The global dynamic maps and the static b map can have different spatial resolutions, and must be labelled accordingly. Comparison of visual ridges alone does not establish cross-backend quantitative agreement.

Pilot M/Gamma/X records verify requested versus native epsilon, unchanged D/B after a repeated material reset, exact drive/record contracts, signed spectra and folding conservation. Same-M m=0 controls retain the same drive clock, record duration and sources, so their unfolded spectra can test whether modulation generates sidebands above finite-window leakage. The control FFT comparison is supporting evidence of actual modulation, not a substitute for a complete sideband-resolved eigenproblem.

Initial pilot failures from converting Meep's complex-valued epsilon readback directly to float occurred before dynamic evolution. Their logs are preserved beside the successful cases. The repaired reader uses the real part of the known real nondispersive material, and the successful native readback agrees to machine precision. No native equations or material parameters were changed by this metadata repair.
