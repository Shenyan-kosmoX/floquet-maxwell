# Independent source review: Jin 2025 SI Figure 5

The target is **official Supplementary Figure 5(a-d)**, PDF page18/printed17,
not main Figure5 and not the 2023 preprint figure with the same number.

I visually inspected the official page and the cropped four-panel figure.
The crop preserves original axes, inverse-coupling titles, colorbars, and the
source lower-right label typo (b instead of d). The observable is inverse
coupling magnitude with a non-quantitative 0/infinity colorbar.

Official SI SectionV explicitly selects the TM-like yellow mode and the
**higher-frequency of the two TE-like modes**. This is a local branch identity,
not a fixed global integer eigenvalue index. At Gamma, the two TE modes are
near-degenerate: use the pair/subspace and mask undefined scalar-branch maps.
The paper itself says its near-Gamma defects arise from this degeneracy.

The actual geometry is fixed at a465nm/h85nm/hole diameter100nm, with silica
below and air above. Main calculation nAlGaAs3.47, sensitivity n3.3, are
separate declared nondispersive Maxwell models. Both index values and silica
n1.46 have verified **preprint Methods** provenance. We retried the official
article HTML and PDF URL on2026-10-19; both return a subscription preview and
neither exposes the final Methods index values. The supplied source contract
therefore does not claim final-material confirmation or full dispersion.

Use unit tensor projection to compare the nodal-line selection rules. A shared
normalization and fixed reciprocal cap are a declared visualization choice,
not a fit of the unpublished paper color scale. No absolute pump value or
conversion efficiency is inferred. The official SFig8 pump0.353GV/m and
chi200pm/V do not establish an identical pump amplitude for SFig5.

This comparison tests physical Maxwell-field projection, tensor components,
relative drive phases and cancellation. It does not certify disordered
modulation, full dynamic radiation, open QNM normalization, Floquet spectra or
Chern numbers. Those are distinct capability tests.
